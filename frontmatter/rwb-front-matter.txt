RWBFrontHeading	Foreword: How This Edition Is Made
RWBFrontBody	This edition of the Radiant Word Bible is built for the way Scripture is actually used: read slowly, searched quickly, quoted exactly, and set beside other texts.
RWBFrontBody	{i}One verse, one paragraph.{/i} Every verse begins its own paragraph. Two translations, or a translation and its original text, can be set side by side, verse by verse, in a word processor, a spreadsheet, a plain-text file or a Bible-study program, without re-cutting the text.
RWBFrontBody	{i}Chapter and verse on every verse.{/i} Each verse begins with its own chapter and verse number, and the name of the book runs at the head of every page of the text, so a page that is photographed, copied or shared still says where it comes from.
RWBFrontBody	{i}Ready for the original languages.{/i} Because every verse stands alone, the text can later be joined, word by word, to the Hebrew and Greek behind it through Strong’s numbering. This edition prints no such numbers; it keeps the text ready for them.
RWBFrontBody	{i}Traditional numbering.{/i} The RWB keeps the traditional English numbering of the King James lineage: 66 books, 1,189 chapters and 31,102 verses. Verses that some modern editions leave out or set apart, for example Matt 17:21 and Acts 8:37, are present and numbered. There is one difference: the closing doxology of Romans stands at Rom 14:24–26, as in the Byzantine Greek text, and not at Rom 16:25–27. The Reader notes list it, and the verses that the oldest manuscripts do not contain.
RWBFrontBody	{i}Plain references.{/i} References are written in the style of the Society of Biblical Literature (SBL), with its short book names and an en dash for ranges (for example Exod 3:2–6; Matt 10:8).
RWBFrontBody	{i}Words of Jesus{/i} are set in dark red (dark grey where printed in black and white). {i}Psalm headings{/i} are printed above each psalm, in italics, and are not counted as verses.
RWBFrontBody	{i}Free to copy.{/i} The text is offered to the public domain (CC0 1.0), and the book is set in Carlito, Noto Sans and Liberation Serif (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The RWB is the scripture only.
RWBFrontBody	The Reader notes, and this text in other formats for study and translation, are published at RadiantWord.app.

RWBFrontHeading	Introduction
RWBFrontBody	What makes the {i}Radiant Word Bible{/i} (RWB) unique and special? The RWB strives to not only be faithful to the most respected translations of the Hebrew and Greek man­uscripts that have been made available, but it strives to also be faithful to the {i}heart and intent{/i} of the Word of God and the {i}principles{/i} which underlie it. For example, the RWB was created with the recognition that there is only one living and true God and that He is eternal and unchanging and His truth is eternal and unchanging. The long-recognized cognitive dissonance between the harsh persona of God expressed in the Hebrew Bible vs the parental, loving God expressed in the New Testament has resul­ting in many contemporary Bibles omitting the Hebrew Bible altogether, or all but the books of Psalms and Proverbs. The RWB addresses this problem uniquely, not by omitting most or all of the Hebrew Bible narratives, but by—wherever possible and appropriate—defaulting to specific interpretations which express the persona of the loving, caring, parental God manifested in and by His son, Jesus. Many called and inspired people in the Hebrew Bible heard from God, recorded their understanding of God’s Word, and faithfully acted upon His directions, but we put our greatest trust in the expression of the living, loving God manifested through Jesus.
RWBFrontBody	It is recognized that before Jesus revealed the heart of the loving parental God to mankind, God expressed his desires, directions and will through his servants, the angels. Angels are cited in many verses of the Hebrew Bible and God’s communi­cation with man by way of His angels may have been far more pervasive than what is expressed in those verses. The angels represented God directly and indirectly. Note Moses’ interaction with the {i}“angel of the Lord”{/i} in the burning bush, who stated: {i}“I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.”{/i} (Exod 3:2–6) Since God is seen as unchanging, any distinction between his expressions through His angels vs through His son Jesus must be attributed to diff­erences between the two; not to a dramatic shift in God’s own persona. Therefore, such controversial Hebrew Bible statements attributed to God as {i}“I will punish those who…”{/i} will be interpreted according to the persona of God which Jesus manifested to us. Such a statement may be expressed in the RWB as {i}“you will surely suffer because of your disobedience…”{/i} recognizing that we bring suffering upon ourselves because of and through our disbelief and/or disobedience. Such alternate translations are actually common among the various versions of the Bible. The RWB chooses the more loving and parental interpretation, viewing it as far more accurate and consistent with the heart and persona of God which Jesus later revealed to us.
RWBFrontBody	The same could be said about the few controversial Hebrew Bible passages which are commonly translated to say that God created “evil”. Scholars disagree about the actual and intended meaning of such statements, with numerous versions of the Bible inter­preting {i}“evil”{/i} in these passages as {i}“calamities”{/i} or {i}“disasters”.{/i} Here again, the RWB will defer to the interpretation which conveys the parental and caring, loving persona of the living God which Jesus revealed to humanity.
RWBFrontBody	Consistency plus accuracy of intent, heart and principle are the goals; not accomm­odating and perpetuating the cognitive dissonance created by the perception of a dra­matically changing persona of the unchanging God. Jesus was humanity’s crystal clear picture window into the heart and personality of the one living God. We will default to his persona.
RWBFrontBody	Such treatments of the text are made sparingly, carefully, and cautiously, so as not to change the message of the Hebrew Bible scriptures but rather to create a more con­sistent expression of God’s Word and heart, faithful to Jesus’ living model and charac­ter. The RWB seeks to enhance consistency and harmony with Jesus.
RWBFrontBody	We sincerely hope the {i}Radiant Word Bible{/i} will help you to build a more intimate and deeply heartfelt relationship with God’s Word and with His heart.
